## Sharp original torus estimate

Let \(D_k=(\sqrt2-1)k_0+k_1\) for \(0\ne k\in\mathbb Z^2\), and \(|k|=\|k\|_\infty\). The product of \((k_1-k_0)+\sqrt2 k_0\) and its conjugate is a nonzero integer. Sorting the positive and negative divisors separately gives
\[
 \sum_{N\le|k|<2N}|D_k|^{-2}\le
 2(16N)^2\sum_{j\ge1}j^{-2}\le1024N^2 .
\]
The weighted sum \(\sum_{k\ne0}|k|^{-4}|D_k|^{-2}\le4096/3<37^2\). The original Haar normalization is one and the multiplier of \(N^{-1}\) is \((2\pi i D_k)^{-1}\). Parseval with \(|k|^4\le k_0^4+k_1^4\) and Cauchy–Schwarz give

\]
Here \(37\sqrt2/(2\pi)^3<1/4\) by \(\pi>3\) and \(2\cdot37^2<54^2\). Restricting the sum to \(|k|\ge N\) with dyadic \(N\) divides the right side by \(N\). The corresponding \(L^2\) bound is
