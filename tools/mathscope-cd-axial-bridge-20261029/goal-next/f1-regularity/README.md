closed pulse rectangle, with \(\|J^dH_w-c-n\|_\infty\le\sqrt2r_0<1/2\).

### Slow partition and seams

\[

 \begin{cases}1&|s|\le1/4,\\
 \sigma(3/2-2|s|)&1/4<|s|<3/4,\\

 \end{cases}
\]
Here \(\phi(x)=e^{-x^{-2}}\) for \(x>0\) and \(0\) for \(x\le0\),
and \(\sigma(x)=\phi(x)/(\phi(x)+\phi(1-x))\) is the analytic
interpretation of the actual source step. For \(x>0\),
\(\phi^{(j)}(x)=P_j(1/x)e^{-x^{-2}}\), where
\(P_{j+1}(z)=2z^3P_j(z)-z^2P_j'(z)\). Every fixed polynomial times
\(e^{-z^2}\) tends to zero as \(z\) tends to infinity. This proves flat
joins at the step endpoints, including for the bump with absolute
value because it is constant near zero. On \(|s|\le7/8\) some translate
in \(\{-1,0,1\}\) lies within \(1/2\), with bump at least \(1/2\).
The denominator square is at least \(1/4\). The omitted translates
vanish there. The quotient extends smoothly across \(\pm3/4\) by the
original zero branch, and all its jets vanish there.

For an explicit uniform bound, \(e^{z^2}\ge z^4/2\) for \(z\ge1\)
gives \(|\phi'|\le4\) on \([0,1]\). The sum of the two \(\phi\) values

## CONDITIONAL

Let the actual input and coefficients of a fixed-chart zero-data ODE
\(\partial_wt=M t+g\) be \(C^r\) on a neighborhood of a slow seam
times the entire closed pulse interval. If the corresponding slow
jets of \(g\) vanish on that seam for every \(w\), the zero-data
variation equations give zero jets of \(t\) there by induction on
parameter derivative order. Pulse differentiation of the same equations
gives the mixed jets through the available differentiability order.
The pressure relation inherits flatness where its denominators are
nonzero and its coefficients and actual input are defined. A mapped
curl uses one more derivative of its potential. The coordinate and
cutoff bounds above supply necessary ingredients for this implication.
