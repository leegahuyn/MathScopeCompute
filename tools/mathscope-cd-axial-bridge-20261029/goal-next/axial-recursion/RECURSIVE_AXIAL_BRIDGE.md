## Original higher weighted axial face recursion

common interior support $[X_-,X_+]$. The original weight is

$$

-\frac{t_1^2}{\log^2(X/X_a)}

$$

Its two logarithms satisfy
$\log(X/X_a)\ge t_1/128>0$ and
$\log(X_b/X)>127$ on this support. In particular
$\zeta^{-1}<\exp(16385)$. For a uniform fixed-derivative bound,

$|\log(z/x)|\le 2\rho_*/x\le t_1/256$ by the convergent

$$
\left|\partial_X^r(\zeta^{-1})(x)\right|
\le r!\rho_*^{-r}\exp(65537).
