## Why the exterior restriction does not supply global C4 jets

Paper page 119 explicitly uses two different terminal regions. If z is
nonzero then q has a positive lower bound, and endpoint bounds of the
actual final potentials control the limit. If z=0 and the point is away
from the origin, the exact exterior heat field supplies the limit. Near
the origin, (10.9) uses flatness of the local residual. A single radial
cutoff midpoint lies only in the second region.

Consequently a finite smooth extension of the five midpoint formulas
would extend a restricted observation, not the original force on K. A
bounded generic Borel/Whitney operator would still require authenticated
global jets and their norms. The finite-extension row can avoid claiming
all-order C-infinity, but cannot omit the spatial scope or left-jet
compatibility in (10.10).

## Reproducibility acceptance can be independent of whole-field completion

N8-08 names editions, commit, constants, basis, domain, time schedule,
quadrature, truncations, precision, tails, residual, energy, camera and
build logs. These should be individually present for each selected run.
A finite periodic run must say that its energy is periodic, its arithmetic
is binary64 and its omitted continuum-mode tail is not certified. A source
core run must retain exact original constants, source error intervals and
its stated spatial restriction. Neither run should silently acquire an
all-space final-source interpretation.

The remaining external acceptance is concrete: pin the final repository
revision and served build, observe a fresh CI execution at that exact
revision in its independent environment, compare selected fixtures/runs
within their stated tolerances, and verify the served export/import/replay
and camera/source metadata. A CI workflow definition or a success receipt
from an earlier revision is insufficient. The final-source existence and
whole-K energy obligations belong to their own still-open rows.
