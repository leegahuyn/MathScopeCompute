# C/D original whole-domain energy identity

On a smooth compactly supported velocity field, let the momentum residual be R and let d = div u. Integration by parts gives:

( E(T)-E(0) )/2 + nu integral |grad u|^2 - integral f.u = integral R.u + (1/2) integral |u|^2 d + integral p d.

This is a conditional identity, not an evaluation of MathScope's source-defined final velocity, pressure or force. For divergence-free fields and zero residual, the last terms vanish and the Lemma 10.4 energy law follows. On the physical periodic torus the boundary contributions vanish by periodicity; the auxiliary Fourier torus is not the physical torus.
