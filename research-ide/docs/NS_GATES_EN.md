# The nine outstanding v54 NS acceptance gates

This is an English account of the **original** acceptance requirements and the verified v54 position. It does not weaken a criterion to obtain completion. N1-05 and N1-06 concern the original proof-checking environment; N3-01 through N3-07 concern a single actual leading profile. The baseline is seven PARTIAL and two BLOCKED criteria. Later evidence belongs in a dated addendum to [current status](CURRENT_STATUS_EN.md).

has now completed with actual exit 0 in the documented explicit-path
environment. The [edition-2 verification report](VERIFICATION_2026_10_10_2_EN.md)
contains the exact command results and current nine-condition table. The

## N3-01 — Simultaneous constant selection

**Already established.** The local modules compute explicit separation, analytic norm, contraction, positivity, and tail constants. The A.21 pressure producer checks some necessary large/small conditions with exact enclosures. These are concrete progress, but their separate parameter sets do not produce a single completed profile.

The original selection order is

$$
M_d\to T_d=e^{M_d}+10\to P_*\to\lambda\to h
\to(\text{matching tolerance},j_0)\to(\delta_*,\sigma_*,\Lambda)
\to T_{sh}\to(C,X_R)\to\text{transition widths}\to N.
$$

**Closure checklist.**

1. Replace every required “sufficiently large/small” choice in A.6, B.33, and B.40 by an explicit inequality or a proved selection theorem.
2. Construct a nonempty simultaneous parameter domain in the required dependency order.
3. Bind every downstream profile and certificate to that one domain and parameter identity.
4. Prove transition widths and reserved patches satisfy their separation conditions after all choices.
5. Preserve failures, such as a finite-axis approximation rejecting a large-pressure branch.

The default gluing `logP=0` and exact pressure/axis `logP=14` cannot be combined as a certified parameter witness.

## N3-02 — Exterior and pressure datum

**Already established.** Source radial schedules, Q evolution, M/J correction, angular bumps, amplitude, heat compensation, and exact A.21 pressure bounds are implemented. Their finite η and high-precision comparisons are retained.

**Unclosed identity.** The A.21 analytic producer defines the ideal target pressure. The exterior routine builds a numerically corrected E. It still must be proved that the corrected E has exactly the same integrated pressure increment for every η. Near agreement at selected points is not this identity.

**Closure checklist.**

1. Use one corrected exterior E and explicitly define every correction and reserved support.
2. Establish E>0 and all required moments on the full η domain.
3. Prove pressure-preserving corrections leave the A.21 target unchanged.
4. Prove A.7 heat compensation restores the datum after the far exterior is replaced.
5. Include both infinite ends, convergence, and differentiation/interchange justifications.
6. Bind the resulting exact datum to the axis producer's exact h and source identity.

## N3-03 — Infinite axis, finite jets, and formal premises

**Already established.** Rational-pressure and actual-A.21 local modules compute invariant-ball, contraction, positivity, and infinite-tail bounds. Their arguments cover a specified local continuous domain, not just samples. The source-axis consumer checks exact h identity.

**Unclosed identification.** An existence/bound statement about an infinite fixed point and a finite η-jet array are two different objects until their relation is established. The package has no automatic transfer of the rational fixture's four scalar Lean statements to all generated A.21 premises.

**Closure checklist.**

2. Instantiate all hypotheses of the chosen original theorem for the actual A.21 datum.
3. Prove the finite recurrence coefficients match the exact coefficients used by that fixed point.
4. Enclose finite radial/η truncation and arithmetic roundoff errors in the same norm.
5. Produce the Lean statements and actual kernel receipts for the generated premises and their connection.
6. Transport those exact data and bounds to the continuation/gluing input, rather than an unrelated numerical endpoint.

V_0=\frac X L\bigl(2\eta U-2D\eta\,\mathcal A_X(U)-d\partial_\eta\mathcal A_X(U)\bigr).

**Closure checklist.**

A small finite residual, by itself, neither proves an exact identity nor produces a solution-error bound without the needed stability estimate.

## N3-05 — Continuous five-moment gluing

**Already established.** The actual B.26 endpoint feeds B.34, the radius scaling, G restoration, and the five cumulative debts. The routine constructs the nonlinear two-U/three-E bump map, its full quadratic terms, raw Jacobian, damped Newton steps, and independent reintegration. Numerical checks pass for the documented finite candidate.

**Unclosed inclusion.** A computed root and sampled nonsingular Jacobians are not a uniform implicit-function theorem. The initial Gauss32 failure demonstrates why a second numerical path and actual continuous quadrature bounds must be distinguished.

**Closure checklist.**

1. Enclose every continuous bump and transition integral, with explicit quadrature and rounding bounds.
2. Bound the nonlinear moment map and Jacobian over a parameter box, including all quadratic terms.
3. Prove uniform invertibility or a validated preconditioned contraction throughout the full required η range.
4. Establish interval-Newton/implicit-function inclusion and keep the solution within allowed bump supports and amplitudes.
5. Enclose the upstream exact-axis/continuation debt; do not certify an independently supplied arbitrary debt.
6. Use the same simultaneous source parameter domain as N3-01, including its large-amplitude conditions.

A validated finite coefficient subproblem can advance this work without certifying the entire source-profile gate. Its quantified domain and its upstream assumptions must be explicit.

## N3-06 — Uniform strict cone after modulation and restoration

**Already established.** C.1/C.12 and first-patch nonlinear restoration are implemented on an explicit annulus. Fixed-state all-phase loop bounds and finite-domain cone samples are recorded, including actual failures at small N and near cutoffs.

**Closure checklist.**

1. Apply the modulation to the same final profile certified by the preceding gates.
2. Prove loop assumptions and η-derivative bounds uniformly for every state of that profile.
3. Bound the O(1/N) value change and order-one radial derivative change on continuous regions, including cutoff collars.
4. Choose one finite N with a proved positive strict cone margin κ across the entire required domain.
5. Prove first-patch moment restoration uniformly and show it preserves the strict margin and reserved-patch properties.
6. Link the annular construction to the regular Cartesian axis and outer region.

The N=512 sample pass cannot replace this all-domain statement. Increasing sampling density without a covering/variation bound does not create a continuous certificate.

## N3-07 — Stress support, flat factorization, and endpoints

**Already established.** Individual components record reserved patches, source flat cutoffs, support locations, derivatives, and safe endpoint handling. Signed logarithms and exact rationals preserve tiny nonzero quantities. Direct `0/0` normalization at an edge is rejected.

**Closure checklist.**

1. Define the actual stress T₀ of the same final field after all corrections.
2. Prove T₀ vanishes outside the designated annulus and is nonzero inside it.
3. Establish the required flat factorization, including the specified exponential cutoff behavior.
4. Derive the edge limit of `n=T₀/|T₀|` from that factorization, without evaluating the undefined ratio at an endpoint.
5. Prove inner/outer flatness and the unchanged power laws on I_pos and I_mean after every correction.
6. Connect all statements to the source edition, final parameter identity, and field revision used by the other gates.

Local support tests are useful component evidence. They do not prove the existence and global support of the completed stress field.

## How the gates can be closed without losing provenance

Each new record should state its exact theorem or interval claim, hypotheses, quantified domain, source/parameter hashes, numerical method where applicable, independent checks, and actual Lean receipt if formal verification is claimed. False examples and failed runs stay in history. Update a criterion only when its original acceptance condition is met; otherwise report the narrower completed subproblem and retain the open parent gate.
