## 4. The minimum faithful mixed-array connection

The original criterion does **not** specify that all 125 numerical
boxes must be reevaluated inside Lean. Requiring that particular new
implementation would add a criterion. The actual existing interval
proof, independent rational arithmetic and kernel identities can be
connected while retaining their distinct scopes.

A sufficient concrete connection has the following ingredients:

- For the selected comparison element and every eta in the original
  window, its nth coefficient is exactly
  `(-chi(h,j,sigma,eta)/2)^n / (n! (n+1)!)`.
- Its derivative identity and the actual selected norm error give,
  for every n and m, the bound on the difference from the nonlinear
  coefficient. The change to xi Taylor coefficients uses exactly
  `j^m/m!`; the derivative-value convention uses `j^m`.
- The finite producer's polynomial for `H(j xi)/j`, rational chi and
  radial recurrence are algebraically these same functions at the
  selected positive h and j. The exact coefficient at n=0 remains one,
  with zero higher eta derivatives.
- The accepted 125 coefficient intervals, 75 mixed values and their
  independent 858-check review retain their exact hashes. Their
  nonlinear, radial-tail, Cauchy eta-tail and rounding bounds are
  attached to those actual functions and derivative conventions.
- The actual Taylor chart `|eta| <= rho/4` is distinguished from the
  comparison-only complex disk `|xi| <= 1/16`. The larger disk is never
  asserted to be a nonlinear solution domain.
- The same selected infinite object is identified with the written
  continuation source by its actual parameters, fixed-point equation
  and uniqueness. A numerical endpoint from a different datum cannot
  substitute for that object.

Once these are supplied and independently checked, a still-false
`full125MixedCoefficientBoxesKernelInstantiatedByThisCheck` flag in an
older component is not itself an open original gate. It records that
component's narrower execution scope. Conversely, a new aggregate
receipt may not erase an actual missing eta-domain or same-object
identification merely because the radial array succeeded.
